Virtual ID — Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Virtual ID is a Twitch panel extension that displays a streamer's profile card: profile details, lore, models, channel emotes and badges. It runs entirely in your browser and has no backend server: the extension itself does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Data the extension uses

  • Broadcaster configuration — everything the streamer enters on the configuration page (texts, colors, image links, Twitch names of related channels) is stored in Twitch's own Extension Configuration Service, under the streamer's account. It contains no viewer data.
  • Viewer identity (optional, with your consent) — if you click Grant access, Twitch shares your Twitch user ID with the extension. It is used only inside your browser, only while the panel is open, to personalize the emotes & badges tab: your subscription tier, your subscription length and your current/next sub badge. It is never stored, logged or transmitted anywhere other than the lookups listed below. If you decline, the panel simply shows the non-personalized view.

Third-party lookups

To display live, public information without a backend, the extension queries the following services directly from your browser. Each request contains only the minimum needed (a channel name, or — after you grant access — your Twitch username):

  • Twitch APIs (api.twitch.tv, gql.twitch.tv, static-cdn.jtvnw.net) — channel emotes, sub & bits badges, category search and box art.
  • decapi.me — whether a related channel is currently live, and its current category.
  • api.ivr.fi — public subscription information (tier, cumulative months, follow status) used for the personalized emotes tab.
  • unavatar.io — public profile pictures of related channels.
  • Cloudinary (res.cloudinary.com) — hosts the images the streamer uploaded for their card (banner, models).

These services receive the requests as any website you visit would; their own privacy policies apply to them.

What the extension does not do

  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.
  • No cookies and no persistent storage in your browser.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • Clipboard access only happens when you click an emote to copy its name, and only writes that emote name.
